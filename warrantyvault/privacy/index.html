WarrantyVault — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 August 2026 · Developer: Mateusz Olczyk (matt.olczyk@gmail.com)

The short version

WarrantyVault is an offline app. Your items, warranty dates and receipt photos are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and we never see, collect or sell the contents of your vault. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising and crash-reporting SDKs described below send — and analytics/ads personalization run only with your consent.

Data stored on your device (never sent to us)

  • Items you add: names, categories, purchase dates, warranty lengths, prices, stores, notes.
  • Receipt photos — copied into the app's private storage; no other app can read them.
  • Your settings (theme, reminder plan).

Backup files you export (items + photos in one archive) are written to the location you choose and are entirely under your control. Claim PDFs you generate are shared only where you send them.

Data that leaves the device

CategoryWhatPurposeRecipient
Advertising Advertising ID, device info, coarse ad-request metadata Showing ads (a banner, an occasional interstitial, an optional rewarded ad). Ad personalization depends on your consent choice. Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)
Analytics Anonymous usage events (e.g. "item saved"), app version, device model Understanding which features are used. Disabled until you give consent in the consent dialog. Firebase Analytics
Crash reports Stack traces, device model, OS version Fixing crashes. Gated by the same consent. Firebase Crashlytics

We do not sell data. We do not share your vault contents with anyone — they never reach us in the first place.

Consent (GDPR / UMP)

On first launch in regions where it is required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent choices for ads. Analytics and crash reporting are enabled only after consent resolves positively. You can change consent through the form shown at first launch (reinstalling resets it).

Permissions

PermissionWhy
INTERNETServing ads and sending consent-gated analytics/crash reports. The vault itself works fully offline.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSWarranty-expiry reminders you configure. Deny it and the app still works — you just get no reminders.

WarrantyVault does not request camera or storage permissions: receipt photos are taken by your system camera app writing directly into WarrantyVault's private storage, and gallery picks use Android's photo picker.

Data retention and deletion

Everything lives on your device: deleting an item deletes its photos, and uninstalling the app deletes the entire vault. Backup files you exported remain wherever you saved them — delete them yourself if you no longer want them. Ad, analytics and crash data are retained by Google according to Google's privacy policy.

Children

WarrantyVault is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13.
