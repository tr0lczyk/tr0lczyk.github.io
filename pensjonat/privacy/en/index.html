Pensjonat — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 August 2026 · applies to the Android app com.tr0lczyk.pensjonat and the owner's web panel

The app is made by Mateusz Olczyk, an individual developer based in Poland. Contact: matt.olczyk@gmail.com.

The short version. This is a work tool for small lodging properties, not an advertising product. There are no ads, we never sell any data, and we do not process hotel guests' data. We process what is needed to organise housekeeping: rooms, tasks, checklists, staff first names and room photos. Everything is stored in the European Union. The app also collects basic telemetry (usage statistics and crash reports) — it is enabled by default, which we state plainly in section 4.

1. Roles: who is responsible for what

The app is a B2B tool used by a lodging property (guesthouse, villa, apartments) and its staff. The roles are therefore as follows:

  • The property owner is the data controller for their staff's personal data (names in the staff roster, task assignments, photos taken as part of work). The owner decides who is added to the roster and which tasks are assigned.
  • We (the app developer) act in that respect as a data processor — we store and process that data solely to make the app work, on the property owner's behalf.
  • For the owner's account data (e-mail address) and for telemetry (section 4), we are the data controller.

If you are a staff member and want your data corrected or deleted, contact the property owner first; you can also write to us.

2. Accounts and sign-in

  • Staff (Android app): no accounts are created. The phone receives an anonymous technical account (a random Firebase Authentication identifier) linked to the property after entering an invite code. We collect no staff e-mails, passwords or phone numbers.
  • Owner (web panel): signs in with an e-mail address and password or with a Google account. We store the e-mail address and account identifier; passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication (Google) — we never see them.

3. What data we process and why

CategoryWhat exactlyWhy
Property data Property name, rooms, cleaning tasks, checklist templates and states, issue reports, invite codes. The app's core function: organising and verifying housekeeping.
Staff roster Staff member's first name or display name, link to the technical account, task assignments. So it is clear who cleans which room and whose phone to notify.
Room photos Photos taken by staff as part of a checklist or an issue report (Firebase Storage). Proof of completed cleaning and issue documentation for the owner.
Push notifications Device tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) stored in the user profile; stale tokens are removed automatically. Delivering notifications about new tasks and rework requests.

We do not process hotel guests' data. The app handles no bookings, check-ins or any data about people staying at the property.

4. Telemetry: usage statistics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports). Collected data includes app usage events, device model, OS and app version, and — on a crash — the state of the app at the time of the error.

Important: telemetry is enabled by default. The app is an ad-free B2B tool — telemetry data is used solely for diagnosing errors and improving the product, never for advertising or marketing profiling. The app does not show a telemetry consent dialog; the legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you do not accept telemetry, write to us — until then, the only complete alternative is not to use the app.

The app contains no ads and does not use the advertising ID for advertising purposes.

5. Where data is stored

The app's backend is Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Cloud Functions, Firebase Cloud Messaging), running on Google Cloud. Property data, photos and server functions run in the europe-central2 region (Warsaw) / in the European Union. Google LLC acts as our sub-processor; data transfers are governed by Google Cloud's standard contractual clauses.

6. Android app permissions

INTERNETSynchronising tasks, checklists and photos with the backend.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSNotifications about new tasks and rework requests (Android 13+; the system consent prompt is shown, and declining does not block work).

The app has no camera and no location permission. Photos are taken with the system camera app; a photo is first saved to the app's private directory and linked to the task only after a successful upload.

7. How long we keep data

  • Room photos: 12 months from upload — then deleted automatically (storage lifecycle rule).
  • Property data (rooms, tasks, checklists, staff roster): for as long as the property uses the app; deleted at the owner's request.
  • Staff technical accounts: until unlinked from the roster (e.g. when a phone is replaced, the old link is cleared) or removed from the roster.
  • Push tokens: stale tokens are removed automatically after a failed delivery.
  • Telemetry: per Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics retention periods (up to several months).

8. What we never do

  • No ads and no cooperation with ad networks.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • No marketing profiling of users.
  • No processing of property guests' data.
  • No collection of location, contacts or phone contents.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict its processing, port it, and object to processing based on legitimate interest (including telemetry). For staff data, the right addressee of a request is the property owner (the controller); for the rest — us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: PUODO).

10. Children

The app is a work tool and is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

11. Changes to this policy

We will announce material changes in the app or on this page by updating the effective date. The current version always lives at https://tr0lczyk.github.io/pensjonat/privacy/en/.
